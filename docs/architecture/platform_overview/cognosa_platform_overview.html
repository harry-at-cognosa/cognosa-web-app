Cognosa Platform Overview

How a question is answered, and what the platform is made of

Cognosa lets an organisation ask questions of its own documents and get answers with sources, using whichever language model it prefers. Each client group has its own knowledge bases, users, instructions, and model choices, kept separate from every other group.
